## Illustrations

Everything in `public/art/*.svg` is generated by `scripts/generate-art.ts` from `scripts/art/*`
(an isometric voxel renderer written for this project). It contains no Mojang or third-party
artwork, textures, models or logos. Regenerate with `npm run art`.
