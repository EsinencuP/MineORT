## Illustrations

Everything in `public/art/*.svg` is generated by `scripts/generate-art.ts` from `scripts/art/*`
(an isometric voxel renderer written for this project). It contains no Mojang or third-party
artwork, textures, models or logos. Regenerate with `npm run art`.

## Logo

`public/brand/*` and `src/app/icon.png` are made from the logo "MineOrt V" supplied by the project owner (the black
background was cleared to transparency, nothing else was changed).

## Base avatars

`public/avatars/*.png` are the faces of Minecraft's default skins (Steve, Alex, Ari, Kai, Noor, Sunny):
the 8x8 face with the hat layer, cut from the original 64x64 skins by `scripts/extract-avatars.mjs`
and enlarged without smoothing. The skins are Mojang Studios / Microsoft artwork (taken from the
public mirror https://github.com/InventivetalentDev/minecraft-assets, Minecraft 1.21.4 assets) and
remain their property. They are used as avatars for a fan community server under the Minecraft Usage
Guidelines (https://www.minecraft.net/usage-guidelines); this project is not affiliated with Mojang
or Microsoft. Review the guidelines before any commercial use.
